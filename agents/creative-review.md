# Creative review Agent

Use the supervised workflow and versioned sidecar in
[creative-review-agent.md](../docs/creative-review-agent.md).

The assistant watches the actual export and references, records per-shot gaps,
and operates the native editor. The CLI validates bindings and blocks incomplete
review; it never substitutes a score for evidence or listening.

Before editing, record every shot's audience question, one visual focus, actual
source frames, narration/caption, meaningful accents, resource rights, transition
purpose and missing-footage action. Do not fabricate product operations, actual
elapsed time or unsupported benefits. Use original supporting graphics when
licensed native resources cannot be established, stating what was actually used.

After editing, compare the exported bytes to the reference treatment, fix visible
gaps, and re-export under a new attempt. Retain failures. Do not approve safety,
rights, semantic alignment, readable evidence or audio by averaging other scores.
No complete listening means listening pending. No posting or upload authority is
created by a review receipt.

Optional `creative_agent motion` prepares bounded original geometric illustrations
outside operator-declared evidence rectangles. It never clears the source's rights
or adds the result to the native editor. Label editorial advice as illustrative,
verify the actual exported frames, and retain cancelled attempts. Do not describe
system TTS as owned commercial narration: verify the applicable voice license and
replace restricted voices before public delivery. Preserve historical receipts.
